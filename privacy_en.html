Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

The "Dotney" application ("Dotney," "the App," "we," "our," or "us") is crafted to help users capture, understand, organize, and collect screenshots, spontaneous thoughts, and clipboard items freely without task deadline anxiety ("Anti-Todo Inspiration Vault").

Because screenshot and personal note content can be deeply personal, this Policy is written to describe in direct, transparent, and rigorous terms what information we process, how artificial intelligence assists in understanding, how data is stored, and your complete rights and choices.

1. Information We Process

Content You Provide & Import:

  • Screenshots and photos you explicitly import or send into the vault via Shortcuts "Teleport".
  • Spontaneous thoughts, notes, and clipboard texts or URLs you create or paste.

Permissions & Device Sources:

  • Photo Library Access: Used exclusively to access screenshots you select or pass via Shortcuts. We never scan your photo library in the background.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition: Used only when you hold the microphone button to dictate a thought locally using iOS's native Speech framework. Audio is transcribed on-device and never uploaded.
  • Apple Reminders (EventKit): Invoked only when you explicitly export an inspiration card to native Apple Reminders.
  • Location (When In Use): Used strictly on-device to compute real-time distance to place cards; we do not track routes or upload location data.

Derived Data & AI Inferences:

  • Text extracted from screenshots via Apple's native Vision OCR framework.
  • Inferred structured tags: 12 inspiration categories (Schedule, Movie, Book, Music, Routine, Place, PromoCode, Wishlist, Profile, Video, Action, Photo).
  • Concise titles, emojis, key takeaways, and action steps (Todos) derived via AI.

Zero-Account Architecture: Dotney requires no sign-up or login credentials. We do not collect phone numbers, email addresses, or social accounts, and we do not link activity to personal real-world identities.

2. How We Use Information & AI Processing

We process information solely to deliver Dotney's core product experience:

  • To synthesize screenshot takeaways into fan-stacked inspiration cards.
  • To automatically categorize items across 12 distinct inspiration vaults.
  • To power lightweight Lock Screen and Dynamic Island companions via Live Activities.

🔒 No Training on Your Content

We do not use your screenshots, notes, extracted text, or any derived data to train or fine-tune artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.
Your content is processed solely to deliver real-time card synthesis to you. Third-party AI providers handle requests under strict developer agreements prohibiting the retention of user content in public training pools.

3. Third-Party AI Model Providers

To provide high-accuracy natural language understanding and 12-tag categorization, the App integrates compliant large language model APIs (such as DeepSeek API):

  • Text-Only Processing: Only extracted plain text snippets are transmitted to infer titles and categories. Raw image pixels are never uploaded.
  • Stateless Processing: API calls are stateless and ephemeral, containing no personal identity tokens. Upon returning structured JSON, data is not stored in ongoing user profiles.
  • Public Metadata Retrieval: For open-source projects or public articles, the App may query public search indices (such as DuckDuckGo snippet summaries or GitHub open APIs) for official URLs. No private user data is involved.

4. On-Device Local Sandbox & User Control

  • On-Device Local Sandbox Storage: All original screenshots, inspiration cards, category tags, and thoughts are stored 100% locally within your device's App Sandbox and local shared container (App Group: group.com.arcade.screenshottodo).
  • Zero Developer Databases: The developer has never deployed, rented, or operated any centralized user database servers. The developer cannot view, access, or hold your private cards.
  • Full User Control & Deletion: You can edit or delete any card at any time. When you uninstall Dotney from your device, all data within the local sandbox is completely and irreversibly erased by iOS.
  • iCloud Sync (Optional): If multi-device sync is enabled, all transmission and storage are managed directly via Apple's official end-to-end encrypted iCloud private containers. The developer has no access to your iCloud data.

5. Zero Third-Party Advertising & Zero Trackers

  • No Advertising SDKs: The App contains zero commercial advertising SDKs (such as AdMob, Pangle, or Tencent Ads).
  • No Tracking Frameworks: The App contains zero user analytics frameworks (such as Firebase, Google Analytics, or Flurry).
  • No Sale of Data: We do not collect Advertising Identifiers (IDFA), unique device identifiers (UUID), or device fingerprints. We never sell or share user data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Automated Processing & Limitations

Dotney utilizes automated algorithms and large language models to generate tags, summaries, and action steps. Outputs may occasionally be incomplete or misclassified. Dotney is designed for personal inspiration and note management; please do not rely on it for critical legal, financial, or medical decisions.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect product improvements or Apple Developer Policy guidelines. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
