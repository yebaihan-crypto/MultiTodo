Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

The "Dotney" application ("Dotney," "the App," "we," "our," or "us") is designed to help users capture, organize, understand, and collect screenshots, inspirations, thoughts, and clipboard items freely without task deadline anxiety ("Anti-Todo Inspiration Vault").

Because screenshot and personal note content can be deeply personal, this Policy is written to describe in direct, transparent, and plain terms what we process, how AI technologies assist in understanding, how data is stored, and your complete rights and choices.

1. Information We Process

  • Content You Provide: Screenshots you import or capture via Shortcuts "Teleport", photos, spontaneous text notes (Thought), and clipboard clips or links.
  • Permissions and Device Sources:
    • Photo Library Access: Used exclusively to access screenshots you explicitly select or send via Shortcuts. We never scan your photo library in the background.
    • Microphone & Speech Recognition: Used only when you press the microphone button to dictate a thought locally using iOS's native Speech engine.
    • Apple Reminders (EventKit): Invoked only when you explicitly export an inspiration card to native Apple Reminders.
    • Location (When In Use): Used strictly on-device to compute distance to place cards; we do not track routes or upload location data.
  • Derived Data & AI Inferences:
    • Text extracted from screenshots via Apple's native Vision OCR framework.
    • Structured signals: 12 inspiration category tags (Schedule, Movie, Book, Music, Routine, Place, PromoCode, Wishlist, Profile, Video, Action, Photo).
    • Refined titles, emojis, key takeaways, and action steps (Todos) derived via AI.
  • Zero-Account Architecture: Dotney requires no user accounts. You are never asked for phone numbers, email addresses, or social login credentials.

2. How We Use Information & AI Processing

We process information solely to provide Dotney's core features:

  • To extract key takeaways from screenshots and generate fan-stacked inspiration cards.
  • To categorize items into 12 distinct inspiration tags and maintain your inspiration vault.
  • To display lightweight companions on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island via Live Activities.

🔒 No Training on Your Content

We do not use your screenshots, notes, extracted text, or any derived content to train or fine-tune artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.
Your content is processed solely to deliver real-time card organization and synthesis to you. Third-party AI providers handle requests under developer agreements prohibiting the retention of user content in public model training datasets.

3. Third-Party Providers & AI Model Providers

To deliver advanced natural language understanding and 12-tag categorization, the App integrates compliant cloud-based AI model APIs (such as DeepSeek API):

  • Text-Only Processing: Only extracted plain text snippets are transmitted to the AI endpoint to infer titles and categories. Raw image pixels are never uploaded.
  • Stateless Processing: API calls are stateless and ephemeral, containing no personal identity tokens. Upon returning structured JSON, data is not linked to ongoing user profiles.
  • Public Metadata Grounding: For open-source projects or public articles, the App may query public search indices (such as DuckDuckGo snippet summaries or GitHub open APIs) for official URLs and metadata. No private user data is involved.

4. Data Storage, Security & User Ownership

  • On-Device Local Sandbox Storage: All original screenshots, inspiration cards, category tags, and thoughts are stored 100% locally within your device's App Sandbox and local shared container (App Group: group.com.arcade.screenshottodo).
  • Zero Developer Databases: The developer has never deployed, rented, or operated any centralized user database servers. The developer cannot view, access, or hold your private cards.
  • Full User Control & Deletion: You can edit or delete any card at any time. When you uninstall Dotney from your device, all data within the local sandbox is completely and irreversibly erased by iOS.
  • iCloud Sync (Optional): If multi-device sync is enabled, all transmission and storage are managed directly via Apple's official end-to-end encrypted iCloud private containers. The developer has no access to your iCloud data.

5. Zero Third-Party Advertising & Zero Trackers

  • No Advertising SDKs: The App contains zero commercial advertising SDKs (such as AdMob, Pangle, or Tencent Ads).
  • No Tracking Frameworks: The App contains zero user analytics frameworks (such as Firebase, Google Analytics, or Flurry).
  • No Sale of Data: We do not collect Advertising Identifiers (IDFA), unique device identifiers (UUID), or device fingerprints. We never sell or share user data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Automated Processing & Limitations

Dotney utilizes automated algorithms and large language models to generate tags, summaries, and action steps. Outputs may occasionally be incomplete or misclassified. Dotney is designed for personal inspiration and note management; please do not rely on it for critical legal, financial, or medical decisions.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect product improvements or Apple Developer Policy guidelines. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
